# What fabrik is

fabrik is a knowledge base for one person, and the routines Claude follows to do your work. You describe how you do a piece of work, and Claude writes it down as a routine. When you ask for that work, Claude does it by the routine. It uses the connectors and plugins you have, such as your email, calendar or accounts.

fabrik stores your routines, records and files in a Supabase project you own. Claude reads and changes them through fabrik's connector, as you, in the Claude desktop app, at claude.ai and in the Claude phone app.

## What it does

- **Your work, by your routines.** Claude finds the routine for the work you ask for, and names the version it follows. It does each step with the connector or plugin the step names. A step that is yours stays yours. See the routines guide.
- **Records.** Notes, decisions, evidence, plans, tasks and models, each kept with every earlier version. See the records guide.
- **Files and search.** PDFs, documents, spreadsheets and notes, found by their words and their meaning together with your records. See the files guide.
- **Projects and tasks.** Work with several steps, planned, tracked and drawn as a board.
- **Contacts and groups.** The people and organisations you deal with, and lists of them.
- **Routines.** How you do a piece of work today, written down, and a better way designed with the connectors and plugins you have or could add.
- **Imports.** Your LinkedIn, Notion, Obsidian, Substack and X exports, brought into fabrik.
- **Email.** A mailbox you set up in fabrik, for a provider with no Claude connector. Claude reads and searches it, and drafts, sends and moves to Trash only what you approve.
- **Drawing and printing.** Boards, graphs and PDFs made from what fabrik holds.
- **Your writing voice.** Writing you mark as your own, so Claude drafts in your voice.

## How it acts

Every tool works as you, within your own grants. Claude shows you each change before it saves it, and waits for you to approve it. Nothing in fabrik runs unattended: Claude acts only while you are in a session, on what you asked for in it.

## Your agent's name

You can give your fabrik agent a name of your own, such as Ada. Say "call yourself Ada" in any Claude app, or give a name when you get started. Claude keeps the name in your profile in fabrik, and uses it whenever it speaks of itself, in every Claude app. A name is 1 to 40 characters on one line. Say "go back to no name" to clear it. Each person names their own agent, and the name changes how it speaks, not what it can do.

## What it is made of

Supabase hosts your database, your files and the functions that run fabrik, including the connector. Cloudflare hosts the page where you sign in. Claude is how you use it, through the connector in every app, and through the fabrik plugin in a Code session in the desktop app.
