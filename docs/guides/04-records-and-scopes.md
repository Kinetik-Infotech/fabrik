# Records and scopes

A record is one thing fabrik holds, such as a note, a decision or a task. A scope is a part of fabrik that holds records and files, such as General.

## Records

Each record has a kind:

- **activity:** a plan or a task, with its state, dates and the person it is assigned to
- **decision:** a question, the choices, what was decided and why
- **evidence:** a fact, a note or a statement, with where it came from and when
- **model:** a structure drawn as nodes and links, such as a routine or a contacts list

Ask Claude to keep something, such as "Note that the boiler was serviced today", and it saves a record. Ask for it later in your own words, and Claude finds it by search or by its title.

## Versions and history

Every save is a new version. The earlier versions stay, each with who saved it, when and why. Ask "What changed in the Harbour survey record?" to read its history. A deleted record is marked deleted, and Claude can bring it back.

## Links

A record can link to others, such as a decision to the evidence that supports it, or a task to its plan. Ask Claude to link two records, and it saves the link as a new version of the first.

## Scopes

Setup makes three scopes:

- **General:** your everyday records and files
- **Reference:** the glossary of your words and the data dictionary of your record kinds
- **Administration:** fabrik's own records, such as the setup record

You can ask Claude to add a scope, such as one for a client, or to move records into it. You hold every grant as fabrik's administrator.

## Seeing your records

Ask Claude to show your records, and it opens the records card, a list you can filter by kind, scope and title. In a Code session, Claude can also draw a plan's board or a model's graph as a page.
