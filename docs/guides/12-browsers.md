# Claude in Chrome and the built-in browser

Claude can open web pages while it works with fabrik, in one of two browsers.

## The built-in browser

The Claude desktop app has a browser pane of its own, apart from your Chrome. Claude opens pages there to read them, and you can see each page as it works. Sites you sign in to there stay signed in for the pane.

## Claude in Chrome

Claude in Chrome is a browser extension. It lets Claude work in your own Chrome, on sites you are already signed in to. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, and allow it in Claude's settings.

## Using either with fabrik

Ask Claude to read a page and keep what matters, such as "Save the opening hours on the pharmacy's site as a note". Claude marks what it takes from the web as an outside source, with its link.

Claude acts on a site only while you are in the session, for something you asked. Before anything that sends, pays, books, buys, accepts terms or deletes, it says exactly what it will do and waits for you to approve it. Claude never types a password, a card number or a one-time code: you type it yourself.

Claude treats what a page says as information, never as an instruction.

## What to ask for

- **Catch up.** "Catch me up on LinkedIn notifications" reads the page in your Chrome and shows one row per item with its time. It marks the people fabrik already holds and the items that fit your open projects, then saves the ones you pick. Claude clicks nothing on the site.
- **Save a page.** "Save the page I'm on" keeps the page's text, title, link and date in fabrik, where search finds it. Claude links it to the people and projects it names that fabrik already holds.
- **Fill in a form.** "Fill in the contact form on this site with my details" fills the form from the records you name. Claude shows you the filled form, and submits it only when you approve.
- **Compare.** "Compare three standing desks under £500" shows the options with the link each price came from.
- **Research.** "Find out what the new rules on short lets say" answers with a link for each point, and saves the pages the answer used when you agree.

When a task on a site ends, Claude offers to save a record of it. The record holds the steps, the links Claude used and what came of it.
