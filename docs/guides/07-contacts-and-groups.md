# Contacts and groups

A contact is a person or an organisation you deal with. A group is a list of contacts, such as a client's team or a board.

## Contacts

Ask Claude to save a contact, such as "Save Ann Lee, the harbour surveyor, ann@example.com". Claude finds a contact by email, telephone or name, and changes one as a new version. A deleted contact drops out of lists and lookups.

Ask "What do I hold on Ann Lee?" for everything fabrik has on one contact: the contact, its groups and the records that mention it and the records it wrote.

## Groups

Ask Claude to make a group, and to add or remove contacts. Ask for a group's members to list them.

## Bringing contacts in

Your LinkedIn connections and Substack subscribers come in as contacts. See the importing guide.
