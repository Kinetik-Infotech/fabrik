# Importing from other apps

fabrik brings in exports from LinkedIn, Notion, Obsidian, Substack and X. Imports run in a Code session in your `fabrik` folder.

## Getting your export

Download your data from each service, and put the export in your `fabrik` folder:

- **LinkedIn:** your data archive, the Basic_LinkedInDataExport folder or zip
- **Notion:** an export as Markdown and CSV, the Export zip
- **Obsidian:** your vault's folder
- **Substack:** your publication's export, with posts.csv
- **X:** your archive, with tweets.js

## What comes in

- **LinkedIn:** your profile, your connections as contacts, and your own posts
- **Notion:** pages as documents, and people, companies, projects and tasks from databases
- **Obsidian:** each note as a document, and its wikilinks as links
- **Substack:** each post as a document marked as your writing, and subscribers as contacts
- **X:** your posts as one document for each month, marked as your writing

Say "Import my LinkedIn export". Claude reads the export, tells you what it will save, and saves it once you agree.

Writing marked as yours teaches Claude your voice, so it can draft in it.
