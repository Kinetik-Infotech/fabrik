Search

Search your files three ways: keyword, semantic and graph.

fabrik runs all three searches at once and merges their lists by reciprocal rank fusion. A result scores more the higher it sits in each list, so a result near the top of two or three lists comes first.

Claude in Chrome

With Claude in Chrome, Claude works in your own browser, on the sites you're already signed in to. Whatever you can do on a website, Claude can do for you: the shopping, the bills, the flights and the bookings. It reads your social media feeds, types into chat boxes and fills in forms. The only limit is the web, and any site that blocks Claude in Chrome.

It asks before it buys, pays or sends anything. Your passwords and card numbers stay with you: you type them yourself. It works in Google Chrome and in Comet.

More plugins

fabrik's marketplace in Claude also has two plugins for life outside work. You install one from the marketplace and start a session. Both are free and open source, and you can build your own plugins from them.

animated.recipes

Recipe videos you can cook along to.

Ask for a dish and Claude writes an original whole-food recipe for your number of servings. Then it draws each step, narrates it and makes the video, with a recipe card to print.

tripagent.me

A trip planner that works like a travel agent.

Show Claude a few hotels you love and it learns your taste. Then it finds places, hotels and flights that suit you, one at a time.

Open source and free

fabrik is open source, under the MIT licence. Anyone can read the code, the setup script and the plugins on GitHub.

It costs nothing to use. You pay Claude for your plan, and Supabase only if you outgrow its free one. A free Supabase project is paused after a week without use, and you can switch it back on.

Why fabrik exists

I'm Joel Smalley on LinkedIn. I run several ventures, a stack of live projects, and the working relationships my income depends on. One of the ventures is Kinetik Infotech: AI automations for CPG businesses, and Claude training.

I built fabrik so I could manage more projects on my own and not lose productivity to routine admin. It's the system I work in every day, and now it's yours too.
