What it does for you

Memory

Tell Claude about a person, meeting or decision once, and fabrik stores it. Ask for it months later, in your own words.

Search

Find any file by a keyword in it, or by what it's about, in your own words.

News

Ask what's new on LinkedIn or any site you're signed in to, and get the short version, with people you know picked out.

Email

Find that message from your accountant while you're out, and reply to it. fabrik reads your mail, and sends only what you approve.

Content

Posts, proposals and replies that sound like you, because they're drafted from what you've already written.

Planning

Tell Claude what you're working on and what's holding things up, and Claude lists what you can get on with next.

Networking

Before a call, ask who someone is and get everything you've noted about them.

Research

Claude reads the sources for you, and fabrik stores the ones the answer came from.

Admin

Forms filled in from what you've already told Claude. You see the form before anything is sent.

Analysis

Prices, products or options weighed up across sites, with evidence.

Publishing

Websites of any size, put live on your own Cloudflare account under your own web address.

Work

In any app Claude has a connector or plugin for, Claude can do the work for you.

Open source and free

fabrik is open source, under the MIT licence. Anyone can read the code, the setup script and the plugins on GitHub.

It costs nothing to use. You pay Claude for your plan, and Supabase only if you outgrow its free one. A free Supabase project is paused after a week without use, and you can switch it back on.

Why fabrik exists

I'm Joel Smalley. I run several ventures, a stack of live projects, and the working relationships my income depends on.

I built fabrik because I needed one place for all of that: what was said, what I agreed, what comes next. It's the system I work in every day, and now it's yours too.

Get started

You'll need three accounts.

  • ClaudeThe AI that does the work. You need a Claude plan and the Claude desktop app.
  • SupabaseStores your data, in a project you own. Its free plan covers light use.
  • CloudflareHosts your websites and forms under your own web address. Its free plan is enough.
